# Master Services Agreement — Quokka Analytics × Acme Corp (fictional demo data)

Signed: March 3, 2026. Account owner: Dana Whitfield (Quokka). Customer contact: Raj Mehta (Acme).

## Pricing
Acme pays $48,000 per year for the Growth plan, billed annually in advance. Pricing is locked for the first two years. Any discount above 10% requires written approval from the Quokka CFO.

## Volume discount
If Acme exceeds 50 seats, each additional seat is billed at $35 per month instead of the list price of $45 per month. There is no discount on the first 50 seats.

## Service level agreement (SLA)
Quokka guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime for the dashboard and API. If uptime falls below 99.9%, Acme receives a 10% service credit for that month. Below 99.0%, the credit is 25%. Scheduled maintenance announced 48 hours ahead does not count as downtime.

## Data retention
Customer event data is retained for 13 months, then deleted. Acme may request export of all data in CSV or Parquet format within 30 days of termination.

## Renewal and termination
The contract renews automatically on March 3, 2027 for one year unless either party gives 60 days written notice. Acme may terminate early only for material breach, such as two consecutive months below the 99.0% uptime level.

## Support
Acme gets priority support with a 4-hour first response time during business hours (9am–6pm Pacific, Monday–Friday). Weekend support is not included.
