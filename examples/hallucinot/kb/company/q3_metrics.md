# Q3 2026 Business Metrics — Quokka Analytics (fictional demo data)

Prepared by Finance, October 6, 2026.

## Revenue
Annual recurring revenue (ARR) reached $6.2M at the end of Q3, up from $5.4M at the end of Q2 (+15% quarter over quarter). Net revenue retention was 112%.

## Churn
Logo churn rose to 5.6% in Q3, up from 4.1% in Q2. The increase was driven mainly by small self-serve customers on the Starter plan after the July 1 price increase from $29 to $39 per month. Enterprise churn stayed flat at 0.8%. No enterprise customer above $25k ARR churned in Q3.

## Customers
Total paying customers: 1,840 (Q2: 1,795). Enterprise customers: 64. Largest new deal in Q3: Northwind Traders at $120k ARR, signed September 18.

## Product usage
Weekly active users grew 9% to 41,300. The new anomaly alerts feature, launched August 12, is used by 38% of enterprise accounts.

## Headcount
Company headcount is 87 at the end of Q3. Engineering is 41 people. The Q4 plan adds 6 engineers and 2 support staff.
