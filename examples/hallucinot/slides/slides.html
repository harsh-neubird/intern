For once, AI fixes human hallucinations.

A team of Drex agents checks every sentence of a meeting, a document or a chatbot answer against what you actually know — and shows the receipt.

Team Hallucinot · Mahak Gupta · HarshNace AI Drexathon · Swarm Intelligence

The problem

  • In meetings, people state wrong numbers, promise what the contract doesn't allow, and read secrets out loud.
  • Sales flyers, chatbot answers and student posters carry the same made-up facts.
  • Nobody checks in real time — the mistake ships, and someone pays to fix it later.

The hard part isn't finding documents.
It's deciding — on every sentence — whether to speak.
