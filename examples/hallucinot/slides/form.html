Brief explanation
Hallucinot: for once, AI fixes human hallucinations. A team of Drex agents listens to a meeting (or reads a document) and checks every sentence against your real sources: wrong facts, risky promises, leaked secrets, to-dos. When agents disagree, Drex arbitrates. Every verdict shows its source. Our wildcard: Hallucinot is judging the Drexathon itself, checking each team's demo against their own repo and scoring the rubric. Built with Drex (every decision), NDI (reads PDFs/slides), Intern (Slack follow-up), DeepInfra and Adobe. Proof: same accuracy as Claude Opus (47/50), 6× faster, ~500× cheaper.
